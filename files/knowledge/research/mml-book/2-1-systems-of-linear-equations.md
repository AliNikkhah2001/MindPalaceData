Linear algebra plays an important role in machine learning and general mathematics. The concepts introduced in this chapter are further expanded to include the idea of geometry in Chapter 3. In Chapter 5, we will discuss vector calculus, where a principled knowledge of matrix operations is essential. In Chapter 10, we will use projections (to be introduced in Section 3.8) for dimensionality reduction with principal component analysis (PCA). In Chapter 9, we will discuss linear regression, where linear algebra plays a central role for solving least-squares problems. Systems of linear equations play a central part of linear algebra.
