21.9 • SUMMARY (21.46) The door is open. ⇒OPEN(door) The decompositional approach asserts that a single state-like predicate associated with open underlies all of these examples. The differences among the meanings of these examples arises from the combination of this single predicate with the primitives CAUSE and BECOME. While this approach to primitive decomposition can explain the similarity between states and actions or causative and non-causative predicates, it still relies on having a large number of predicates like open.
