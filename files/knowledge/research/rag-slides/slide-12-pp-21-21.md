# Slide 12 (pp. 21–21)

§Slide 12 (pp. 21–21) · pp. 21–21 · RAG: Paradigms, Technologies, Trends (Slide Deck)

## Extrait
Techniques for Better RAG -- Structured Corpus ● Summary → Document Replace document retrieval with summary retrieval, not only retrieving the most directly relevant nodes, but also exploring additional nodes associated with those nodes. Hierarchical Organization of Retrieval Corpora ● Document → Embedded Objects Documents have embedded objects (such as tables, charts), first retrieve entity reference objects, then query underlying objects, such as document blocks, databases, sub-nodes.
