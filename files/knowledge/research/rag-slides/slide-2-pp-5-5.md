# Slide 2 (pp. 5–5)

§Slide 2 (pp. 5–5) · pp. 5–5 · RAG: Paradigms, Technologies, Trends (Slide Deck)

## Extrait
A typical case of RAG When answering questions or generating text, it first retrieves relevant information from a large number of documents, and then LLMs generates answers based on this information. By attaching a external knowledge base, there is no need to retrain the entire large model for each specific task. The RAG model is especially suitable for knowledge-intensive tasks. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
