# Slide 21 (pp. 36–36)

§Slide 21 (pp. 36–36) · pp. 36–36 · RAG: Paradigms, Technologies, Trends (Slide Deck)

## Extrait
Prospects - Existing Challengs of RAG Long context • Retrieved content is excessive, exceeding window limit. • The context is too long to result Lost in the Milddle. • If the context window is not limited, is there still a need for RAG? Robustness • How to handle the incorrect content retrieved • How to filter and verify the content retrieved. • How to improve the model's resistance to toxicity and noise Coordination with FT • How to simultaneously leverage the effects of RAG and FT. • How do the two coordinate, how are they organized, is it in Pipeline, alternating, or end-to-end? Engineering Practice • How to reduce the latency of retrieving ultra-large-scale corpora. • How to ensure that the content retrieved is not leaked by large models The role of LLMs • LLM can be used for retrieval (LLM generation replaces retrieval, retrieving from LLM memory), for generation, and for evaluation. How to further explore the potential of LLM in RAG. Scaling Law • Does the RAG model satisfy the Scaling Law • Does RAG exhibit, or under what scenarios does it exhibit an Inverse Scaling Law Further address the challenges faced by RAG itself
