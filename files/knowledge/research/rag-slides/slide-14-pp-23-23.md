# Slide 14 (pp. 23–23)

§Slide 14 (pp. 23–23) · pp. 23–23 · RAG: Paradigms, Technologies, Trends (Slide Deck)

## Extrait
Techniques for Better RAG -- KG as a Retrieval Data Source GraphRAG Extract entities from the user's input query, then construct a subgraph to form context, and finally feed it into the large model for generation. Implementation Use LLM (or other models) to extract key entities from the question. Retrieve subgraphs based on entities, delving to a certain depth, such as 2 hops or even more. Utilize the obtained context to generate answers through LLM.
