### Grammar 1

| Subject   | **(voir)** </br> (to see) | **(Cuisiner)** </br> (to cook) | **(Chercher)** </br> (to look for) |
| --------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Je        | **Vois**                  | **Cuisine**                    | **Cherche**                        |
| Tu        | Vois                      | Cuisine**s**                   | Cherche**s**                       |
