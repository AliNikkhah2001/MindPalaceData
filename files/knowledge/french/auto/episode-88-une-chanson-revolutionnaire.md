# Une chanson révolutionnaire

Episode 1 · Histoires · A1–A2 · [[index-francais]]

## Extrait (transcript, 8 lignes)
| French | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon ami s’appelle Amine Al-Ghozzi, et c’est un poète extraordinaire. | My friend’s name is Amine Al-Ghozzi, and he is an extraordinary poet. | `s’appeler` means to be called. |
| Il m’a donné un bout de papier, et il m’a dit : | He gave me a piece of paper and said to me: | `m’` means me or to me. |
| J’ai quelque chose pour toi. | I have something for you. | Learn `quelque chose` as one unit. |
| C’est un poème que j’ai écrit. | It is a poem that I wrote. | `que` replaces the object. |
| J’aimerais que tu le chantes. | I would like you to sing it. | `le` replaces the poem. |
| J’ai grandi en Tunisie. | I grew up in Tunisia. | `grandir` means to grow up. |
| Dans mon pays, en 2007, c’était la dictature. | In my country, in 2007, it was a dictatorship. | `c’était` gives past background. |
| Ben Ali était devenu président après un coup d’État. | Ben Ali had become president after a coup d’état. | An earlier event in the past. |
