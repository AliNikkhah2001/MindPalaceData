# Traditions iconiques - Perfume

## Pourquoi ce nœud — explication
Perfume as liquid memory: noses, harvests, and the Grasse basin. Core lesson: senses vocabulary plus artisan verbs (composer, macérer, distiller).
Focus: Craft / perfume / Grasse · Level A2–B1 · [[index-francais]]

## English synopsis (résumé en anglais)
Perfume as liquid memory: noses, harvests, and the Grasse basin. Core lesson: senses vocabulary plus artisan verbs (composer, macérer, distiller).

## Idées clés
1. The nose (le nez): a trained profession, not a gift.
2. Harvest calendar: jasmine, rose de mai, and fragile supply chains.
3. Composition as writing: top, heart, and base notes like paragraphs.

## Point de grammaire
Sensory verbs + direct objects (`sentir`, `respirer`, `composer`). — see also the linked grammar hub.

## Vocabulaire clé
- **le parfum** — perfume (masculine)
- **le nez** — nose (perfumer) (un grand nez)
- **la récolte** — harvest (jasmine harvest)
- **composer** — to compose/blend (composer un parfum)
