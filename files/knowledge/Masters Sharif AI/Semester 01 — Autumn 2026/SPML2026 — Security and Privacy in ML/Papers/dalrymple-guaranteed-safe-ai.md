# Towards Guaranteed Safe AI

> Aspiration for AI systems with **provable** safety properties, not only empirical red-teaming.

## Why this paper (for SPML)
Motivates why SPML cares about certificates, DP guarantees, and architectural isolation later in the course.

## Core ideas
- Distinguish empirical robustness from formal/guaranteed safety.
- Safety requires specification + mechanism + evidence.
- Useful lens for agents and LLMs where failure modes are open-ended.

## How to study it
Framework sections; don’t drown in every proposal—extract the vocabulary of guarantees.
