# Adversarial Patch

> A printable patch in the scene can dominate the classifier—physical, localized attack.

## Why this paper (for SPML)
Makes AEs concrete in the real world (stickers, signs).

## Core ideas
- Optimize a patch under location/scale/rotation expectation.
- Universal across scenes once printed.
- Threat model shifts from ℓ_p balls to localized corruption.

## How to study it
Expectation-over-transform training of the patch; demos.
