# Intriguing Properties of Neural Networks

> Deep nets are discontinuous in practice: tiny input changes can flip predictions (adversarial examples).

## Why this paper (for SPML)
Origin story of adversarial examples; every Module 1 lecture builds on this observation.

## Core ideas
- High-dimensional decision boundaries are brittle.
- Adversarial perturbations can be nearly imperceptible.
- Blind spots are not just random noise sensitivity.

## How to study it
Definition of adversarial example + early L-BFGS crafting; note limitations vs later FGSM/PGD.
