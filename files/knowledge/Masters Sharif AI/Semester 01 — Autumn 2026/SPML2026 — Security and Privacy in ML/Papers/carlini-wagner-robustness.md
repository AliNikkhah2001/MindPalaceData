# Towards Evaluating the Robustness of Neural Networks

> Many defenses look strong only because evaluations are weak; C&W optimization attacks break them.

## Why this paper (for SPML)
Teaches rigorous attack evaluation—mandatory mindset for Module 1 defenses.

## Core ideas
- Formulate AE search as constrained optimization.
- High-confidence adversarial examples.
- Distillation and other early defenses fail under C&W.

## How to study it
Attack formulations (ℓ_0/ℓ_2/ℓ_∞) and the evaluation philosophy more than every hyperparameter.
