# Towards the Science of Security and Privacy in Machine Learning

> Security/privacy for ML needs a threat-model science: assets, adversaries, and measurable guarantees—not one-off attacks.

## Why this paper (for SPML)
Course framing paper. Use it to classify every later attack as integrity, confidentiality, or availability under a clear adversary.

## Core ideas
- Separate **training-time** vs **inference-time** threats.
- Map attacks to CIA goals on model, data, and predictions.
- Prefer evaluations that state adversary knowledge/capability explicitly.

## How to study it
Tables/figures that taxonomize attacks; skim case studies for vocabulary you will reuse all semester.
