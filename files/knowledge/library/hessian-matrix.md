# Hessian Matrix

> **Source**: [[pdf:Matrix Calculus.pdf#page=20|Matrix Calculus.pdf (p. 20)]]

## Definition & Core Intuition
Square matrix of second-order partial derivatives describing local curvature and critical points.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$
H_{ij} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_i \partial x_j}
$$
